# Surface brief: portfolio home (lib/main.dart)

Scope: the single-page portfolio. Mode: Experience (the work leads), with a Persuade close (contact).
Audience: recruiters/hiring managers for senior Flutter roles and founders hiring a contractor — rapid credibility scan.
Action: reach out (email/form/WhatsApp/LinkedIn) or download the CV.
Proof: config.json case studies, CV-backed timeline and metrics. No invented claims.
Pinned by user (2026-09-30): take ideas/colours/design from https://aliradwan.github.io/AliRadwan.portfolio/ ("Embers": warm-dark, one accent, hairlines, big tight type, years chart, inverse contact band) and exceed it; own palette, large hero portrait, keep dark+light toggle.

## Direction contract

THESIS: An engineer's field ledger — evidence set in big confident type and drawn as honest charts (years, users per product), not cards of adjectives. Refuses the category default of a centred avatar + neon glow + icon-tile grids.

OWN-WORLD: "Moss Night": olive-black ground #0E110C, bone ink #EEF0E4, one phosphor-lime #C5F04A used as a solid fill with dark ink on it (buttons, chart bars, dots) and as thin rules; light mode "Bone Day" #F3F2EA with deep-moss #4D6B00 for accent text. Bricolage Grotesque display (w500, tight negative tracking), Hanken Grotesk body/labels. Hairlines, 6px radii, full pills, arrow-circle "go" motif, sweep-fill buttons. No glass, no glow, no gradients except photo scrims.

STORY: Name and role at poster scale → the numbers that prove scale → a career drawn as a chart → the experience ledger → case studies from config → the toolbox → how AI fits the workflow → an inverse bone band that asks for the conversation.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Two columns on desktop (1.25fr/1fr). Left: H1 "Abdallah" / "Ali Rehab," / "Senior Mobile" / "Engineer." (last line lime) revealed line by line from behind masks; one positioning sentence; CTA row: lime "Download CV", outline "View work", arrow circle; socials + email. Right: 4:5 portrait (moss multiply grade) with bottom scrim; caption carries the live-dot "Now · Senior Mobile Engineer at MEGAMIND IT Solutions" and "Cairo, Egypt". Under it a quiet hairline ledger line of where the work happened. Amended 2026-10-04: no status pill above the H1 (craft-floor eyebrow ban) and no metric strip at the fold (craft-floor hero-metric template) — the numbers live in the Scale instrument where each is read against its product. Mobile: text first, portrait after, because the recruiter scan reads the name and role first.

FORM: user-pinned reference world (warm-dark editorial engineer portfolio), own palette; concept roll a0b6e9f3 superseded by the pin. Raise from declined challenger "VU-meter bridge": the discipline of a row of identical calibrated instruments read at a sweep — the users-per-product chart is one instrument: shared 0–4M axis, true proportional bars, and each product's measured outcomes (from its own config impact list) set on its row.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
